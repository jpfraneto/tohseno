01 · The Shot

The identity is not its current form.

A Shot begins when a person signs a commitment to bring a coherent intention into reality. Its random ShotID survives changes to its name, folder, repository, app, platform, deployment, description, and optional token associations.

Origin

Intention preserved

The original human material remains part of origin. Operational refinement never silently replaces it.

Continuity

Genome explicit

The accepted genome says what must remain true. A change to it is proposed and authorized, never hidden in an implementation edit.

Experience

Versions exact

Every accepted expression state has an immutable Version ID. Feedback binds to the exact state that produced the experience.

Portable

Lineage verifiable

Signed deterministic actions let another implementation inspect, replicate, and verify what it actually possesses. Materialization additionally requires trusted source, authority, and acceptance.

02 · Canonical objects

A small protocol vocabulary.

The protocol keeps creative identity, material expression, authority, and economics separate enough to verify each relationship honestly.

  1. Coherent Intention

    The human declaration that something should exist.

  2. Shot

    The persistent identity created when that intention is committed into reality.

  3. Genome

    The current operational interpretation of what must remain true.

  4. Expression

    One concrete manifestation of the Shot; the first factory produces native Apple software.

  5. Version and Feedback

    One immutable expression state, and experience attached only to that exact state.

  6. Evolution and Lineage

    An authorized transition, and the verifiable history connecting origin, versions, and change.

  7. Ownership

    The authority to approve continuity; it is not authorship of every generated line.

  8. Token Association

    An explicit chain-specific economic relationship that never replaces Shot identity or ownership.

Anky can be its own independently owned Shot and associate `$ANKY` on Base (`eip155:8453`) without making the token contract the Shot, changing its controller, merging Anky with TOHSENO, or conflating `$ANKY` with any `$TOHSENO` association. No token address is asserted here.

03 · Privacy

Partial knowledge is honest.

Lineage may identify an artifact without publishing it. Records distinguish absent, unknown, intentionally private, locally available, publicly committed, replicated, cryptographically verified, and on-chain anchored material.

Private by default

  • raw intention unless deliberately published
  • feedback, references, source, and local app data
  • agent plans, transcripts, and working memory
  • private keys, device inventory, and recovery material

Explicit public checkpoint

  • an ancestry-free public checkpoint chain
  • Shot ID, public checkpoint head, controller, and count
  • no ordinary private-lineage digest or runtime data
  • optional token relations remain private until separately projected

04 · Nodes

Nodes preserve records, not truth by decree.

The candidate node validates and content-addresses signed records, rebuilds the state it can justify, reports missing parents and artifacts, and explicitly synchronizes with peers. It stores records, not referenced artifact bytes. A valid partial segment remains authority-unresolved until its causal context arrives. Nodes may hold different subsets and branches; the protocol invents no distributed consensus. Contract checkpoints stay outside node inventory until the receipt-aware public workflows exist.

05 · First factory

Native Apple software is the first expression.

The first TOHSENO factory turns one coherent intention into a native Apple application that its owner can use and evolve. The Apple Fascia provides bounded software organs; deterministic gates—not an LLM's opinion—decide whether a version is accepted.

Current boundary

A stable local product; the public registry product comes next.

TOHSENO ships the local factory and frozen offline verification materials. The retired v0.7 contract generation will never be deployed. The 0.8.0 contract generation is deployed and active on Robinhood Chain under a threshold-signed release authority; the public registry workflows that would write to it are still being built. Portable imports are verified record projections and are not source materializations or ownership transfers.
