Your app. In someone else’s hands. **Menlo is a permissionless distribution network for iOS apps.** Publish your source, share your app’s page, and let another person verify, build, and sign it on their Mac for their iPhone.

Menlo runs on Tohseno. The tools, CLI commands, protocol identifiers, and existing URLs still use that name.

## From your repo to their iPhone

```text
publish source → approve in Companion → share the app’s link
        → recipient verifies source → Xcode build → their signature → their iPhone
```

You need a Mac, Xcode, your own Apple signing identity, and an intended iPhone. Companion holds the Builder’s signing authority. Receiving a release does not skip Apple provisioning, Trust, Developer Mode, or local installation checks.

## One sponsored upload

Menlo sponsors **one upload per Builder**. Retries of the same publication job share that reservation. Later uploads, including Updates, require ETH funding. The cap is deployed, but the paid-wallet setup and address/Copy interface are **not available yet**; later uploads currently stop with a funding-required message.

[Check current availability](/guide/reference/current-status/) before publishing. [Download Menlo for Mac](https://tohseno.com/download/macos) or [explore real apps](https://tohseno.com/registry).
