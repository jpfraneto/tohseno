How it works

Two commands. One approval.
Software moves.

Start with an ordinary Xcode project. Tohseno packages the exact source, asks you to authorize the release, and gives you a link.

01

tohseno init

Connect an existing Xcode project. Tohseno leaves the repository and signing settings yours.

02

tohseno deploy

Prepare the exact public source and release action. Tohseno shows what will be shared.

03

Approve in the Companion app.

Companion’s private DeviceKey authorizes the exact release. Nothing becomes public without that approval.

04

Share the link.

Another Mac verifies the source, builds it in Xcode, and signs it with its owner’s Apple identity.

The distribution model

A link is the channel.
People are the network.

Tohseno removes App Store submission and review from this person-to-person path. A developer publishes buildable source and proof; another developer independently builds it.

The server can move bytes and relay constrained actions. It cannot impersonate a Builder, approve a release, or sign an app for its recipient.

What it changes

Skip App Store submission.
Keep Apple’s signing security.

There is no App Store submission or review in this path. There is still Xcode, provisioning, Developer Mode, Trust, and an Apple signature on every installed app.

Each person needs a Mac running macOS 14 or later, Xcode, an iPhone, and an Apple Account. The sender also approves public releases in Tohseno Companion.
