# Daily Challenge

This bundled app skill supplies the first complete local play loop. Preserve
`DailyChallengeEngine` as the deterministic score calculator and keep the
five-decision run playable before adding content breadth, remote scenarios, or
account mechanics.

The installed scenarios are neutral sample data. Replace their presentation
and content to fit `SHOT.md`, but do not introduce a remote daily source unless
the shot contract and manifest explicitly declare that data movement.
