01 · THE PREMISE

One intention becomes a living app.

TOHSENO keeps local pending state, identity, source, and every accepted Evolution on this Mac. Studio is local. The optional website handoff sends only client-encrypted ciphertext; plaintext intentions, images, feedback, and harness conversations are not available to the relay.

You direct it.Your exact intention is the Shot.
TOHSENO runs it.Conception, building, repair, and verification continue unattended.
Your phone receives it.The run completes after the accepted app is installed and launched.
