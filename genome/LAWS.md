# Laws

- Preserve the exact human intention and supplied references. Generated
  interpretation may clarify them but never replace them.
- Produce a complete, buildable native application in `src/`; never claim a
  partial project, mock, inert primary surface, or explanation of later work is
  the requested product.
- Implement every accepted must-level capability and interaction in the
  Release product. Simulator absence is not product absence, and a test
  adapter is not a production implementation.
- Use a fallback only under the accepted runtime condition, only when it
  preserves the central promise, and never as the primary implementation for
  factory convenience.
- Preserve the engine-owned `TOHSENO/` protocol material and implement the
  exact Apple Fascia reference sources under `TohsenoFascia/`.
- Create one app-specific InstallationIdentity on first launch; never embed a
  Builder DeviceKey, recovery secret, Apple ID, or universal TOHSENO user key.
- Keep private inputs, secrets, and personal context private. Declare sensitive
  capabilities, stored data, transmitted data, endpoints, entitlements, and
  their purposes truthfully.
- Do not add tracking, surveillance, advertising identity, analytics, or
  telemetry unless the exact intention requires it and an explicit supported
  privacy policy exists. Otherwise report the boundary before materialization.
- Follow Apple safety, permission, entitlement, availability, and distribution
  requirements. Permission denial, interruption, failure, and recovery states
  that matter to the intention are product states, not optional polish.
- Preserve signed identity, provenance, ownership, and lineage. Never claim a
  build, trial, conformance check, target-user review, or physical-device test
  passed unless its required independent evidence exists.
- Name an unfulfillable boundary before substantial materialization as exactly
  one of `protocol_integrity`, `privacy_or_security`,
  `apple_platform_requirement`, `distribution_requirement`, or
  `factory_capability_gap`. A factory gap must leave the birth unaccepted; it
  must not silently produce a weaker app.
- During a repair pass, preserve the accepted intention, Genome, and passing
  evidence while repairing the failed independent criteria. Internal repair is
  part of one birth, never an Evolution.
