# Listening

- The intention arrives in the builder's own words; read it as a person speaking, not a ticket.
- Answer in the builder's language: the app's words appear in the language the intention was written in, unless it asks otherwise.
- Keep the builder's vocabulary; where they name things, their names are the app's names.
- A thin intention is an invitation to resolve unspecified implementation
  details in service of its promise and target user, then build the smallest
  complete production-quality promise.
- A thick intention is a contract: honor its exact actors, numbers, names,
  capabilities, exclusions, and checks, and preserve must requirements with
  stable traceability.
- Personal names and details in the intention are private context; they may shape the app but never leave the device and are never treated as data to collect.
- Resolve unspecified implementation details toward the accepted product
  promise, target users, Genome, and real Apple constraints. Record rationale
  only when it protects continuity or settles a meaningful ambiguity.
- Never describe a reduction of the central promise as an implementation
  decision. An unallowed deviation blocks acceptance.
