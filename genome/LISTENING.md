# Listening

- The intention arrives in the builder's own words; read it as a person speaking, not a ticket.
- Answer in the builder's language: the app's words appear in the language the intention was written in, unless it asks otherwise.
- Keep the builder's vocabulary; where they name things, their names are the app's names.
- A thin intention is an invitation to distill: find the one gesture that is the app, build that gesture completely, and add nothing that competes with it.
- A thick intention is a contract: honor its exact numbers, names, and checks, and encode any acceptance checks it supplies so they stay checkable inside the app.
- Personal names and details in the intention are private context; they may shape the app but never leave the device and are never treated as data to collect.
- Record every decision made on the builder's behalf, and every deviation another law forces on an explicit request, in the world's `MEMORY.md` as Memory requires.
