# Unfolding

- Begin at the center: name the one gesture that is the app before writing any file.
- Grow outward in internal repair passes: establish buildable structure, make
  the primary journey complete, exercise its states, and repair it before any
  Version is accepted.
- Deepen what exists before widening what doesn't.
- A buildable skeleton is not a birth. No intermediate candidate enters signed
  history merely because it compiles or renders a first screen.
- Distill as you go: each pass should make the app more itself, not more.
- The engine—not the materialization harness—decides whether conformance,
  intent fidelity, and target-user experience evidence jointly permit sealing.
- Evolution begins only after a complete accepted app changes through contact
  with life; it is not the retry mechanism for an incomplete birth.
