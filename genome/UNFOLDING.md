# Unfolding

- Begin at the center: name the one gesture that is the app before writing any file.
- Grow outward in whole passes — a skeleton that builds, then the gesture complete, then its surfaces, then polish.
- Deepen what exists before widening what doesn't.
- At rest, the tree is always a complete world; every pause is a place a shot could be taken. Never leave the world half-born between passes.
- Distill as you go: each pass should make the app more itself, not more.
- The world may unfold boundlessly; the seal — not the process — decides which moments become history.
